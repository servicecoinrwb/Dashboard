Model assumptions — argue with these

Demand: weekday leads cluster ~58% in the 7–10am burst, ~27% in the 3:30–7pm window, rest scattered. Weekend leads run an all-day plateau 9am–7pm. Volume scales with temperature. The two lead sliders are your LSA budget throttle — low weekdays, high weekends.

Thin competition: after 4pm and all weekend, win odds get a 1.35× boost (capped 92%) — half the vans went home.

A · Traditional: both trucks M–F 8–4, Jason's mornings carry FM (8–11). Complete-the-job visits, regular rates only. No deposit, no confirm texts: 15–25% ghost, 60% of ghosts roll a truck (dry run). After-3pm callers get "tomorrow" (10% stick). Weekend LSA off — no weekend leads received or paid.

MT · Complete & Restart: weekdays — Brian 8–1 then 4–8, Jason 8–4 with FM moved to 1–4. Both complete the job in one visit at regular rate in the morning; every call after 4pm is run that evening at emergency rates (uplift on the whole invoice, diagnostic included). If the part isn't on the truck, the repair returns first slot next morning as a pre-sold job — customer already paid the diagnostic in person and approved the quote, so only ~3% fall through. Weekends: one truck 8am–8pm Sat & Sun, everything at emergency rates. Playbook stack (paid diagnostic + confirm texts): 5% ghost, 90% caught before the truck rolls. LSA pauses when the board is full.

Money: $89 diagnostic on every completed visit ($89 + uplift on emergency calls), repair ticket ±40% around the average, uplift applied to the full invoice on evening/weekend work. FM billed $140/hr, 3 hrs every weekday in both schedules — the gap is capture, waste, and rate, never dropped FM.

Owner hours assume you and Jason alternate weekends, so half the weekend truck-hours count as yours.

Fairness: each simulated weekday customer's patience, ghost tendency, close decision, parts-on-truck, and ticket size are drawn once and shared by both schedules. Weekend customers exist only for MT because Traditional's weekend LSA is off.
